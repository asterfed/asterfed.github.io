About Asterfed

Asterfed is a digital product company under PT Aster Inovasi Teknologi. We develop mobile games and applications with a product-first mindset, grounded in user understanding, market validation, engaging experiences, and sustainable business models.

From mobile games to everyday applications, we turn promising ideas into products people enjoy, value, and return to.
